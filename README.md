# IU-M9 ICT Fest — Cultural E‑commerce Project

This repository contains the 1st-semester ICT project assigned by our university. In this project, we are building a focused E‑commerce website that showcases and sells clothing and accessories inspired by Pakistani culture.

## Why this project

- **Focus:** unlike general marketplaces that try to sell everything, our store specializes in clothing and accessories rooted in Pakistani traditions — from everyday wear to festive attire and handcrafted cultural items.
- **Audience:** the site is designed for both Pakistanis and foreigners who want authentic, high-quality cultural items.
- **Goal:** to deliver a better, more authentic and user-friendly shopping experience than existing Pakistani e‑commerce offerings by combining careful curation, cultural authenticity, and a simple, pleasant shopping flow.

## What makes it unique

- **Curated catalogue:** every item is selected to represent Pakistani culture — fabrics, embroidery styles, regional accessories, and handcrafted goods.
- **Cultural-first design:** product pages, imagery, and descriptions highlight the cultural background and craftsmanship behind each piece.
- **Built with care:** developed with love by Abdul Rehman and his team, the project focuses on authenticity, quality, and user experience.

## Project scope

- **Core features:** product catalogue, product detail pages, shopping cart, checkout, user accounts, and basic order management.
- **Platform:** a responsive web application suitable for desktop and mobile.
- **Non-goals for this semester:** we will not aim to be a full marketplace for every product category; the focus remains on clothes and cultural accessories.
