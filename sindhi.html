Sindhi Collection

Celebrate the geometric blocks of block-printed Ajrak, detailed handloom Sussi weave, and exquisite mirror work from the historical land of Sindh.

The Legacy of Sindh

Dating back to the ancient Indus Valley Civilization, Sindhi traditional accessories represent deep hospitality and pride. The signature block printing process (Ajrak) and mirror work (Topi) represent precision and an artistic celebration of nature.

Geometric Ajrak Blocks

Created through a complex 16-step block printing process using natural indigo dyes.

Mirror-Work Topi

Hand-woven caps decorated with mirrors reflecting Sindh's bright desert sunlight.
